Temi: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Temi is a hobby Discord bot run by [YOUR NAME OR HANDLE] ("the operator") on a personal device. This explains what she stores and how to remove it.

What she collects

Only in the channel(s) she is set up in:

Messages you post: the text, used so she can learn words and conversation, and to remember what was said.
Your Discord user ID and display name, plus simple counters: when you were last seen, how many messages you sent, how much she "likes" you, and whether you asked her not to tag you.
Pictures you attach: she does not keep the original image. She keeps a tiny 32x32 thumbnail, numbers describing its style, the words from your caption or file name, and who showed it. She never opens links people paste.
Reactions and replies to her paintings, which teach her what people like.
What she does not do
She does not read other channels, direct messages or voice, and does not collect email addresses, IP addresses or location.
She does not sell or share your data, and does not repost your pictures or share your messages outside the channel. She can repeat words she has learned in her own sentences.
Where it is stored

On the operator's own device, in files the operator can read, and in occasional backups. Discord itself processes messages under its own policy. To look up words and pictures she contacts public sources (Wikipedia, the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago, NASA, Wikimedia Commons, Openverse). Those requests contain only a search word, never your name or ID.

How long

Until you or the operator remove it, or the project ends.

Your choices
!temi nopics: she stops looking at your pictures (!temi pics undoes it).
!temi hush: she won't tag you.
!temi forgetme: deletes your stored memories and your pictures, and her painting style is recalculated without them.
Or ask the operator (contact below) to delete your data.

One honest limit: Temi is a learning system. Words she has already learned, and what her eyes and brain learned in general from many messages and pictures combined, are mixed into her and cannot be taken back out. Deleting your data removes the stored records, not those general traces.

Children

Temi is for people allowed on Discord (13+, or higher where your country requires). If you believe a child's data is stored, contact the operator and it will be removed.
